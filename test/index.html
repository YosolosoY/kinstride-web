Which lineage do you walk like?

Eight questions about how you really walk. One minute, no sign-up, and the answer comes from the same rules the Kinstride app uses on real walks.

About this test

How does it work?

Each answer estimates something Kinstride measures on a walk, like pace, stride or climb. Those estimates go through the rules of the app, and the lineage closest to you wins.

Is it as accurate as the app?

No. The test trusts your memory. The app measures four weeks of real walks with GPS and your steps, so your lineage there may be different, and it can change when your walking changes.

Do you save my answers?

No. Your answers stay in your browser. We only count how many people start the test and which lineage they got, with no name and no cookies.
