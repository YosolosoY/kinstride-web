KINSTRIDE

Every step takes you farther

Your real kilometres travel across the Farwander, a hand-drawn world of legendary roads. And the way you walk reveals your lineage: dwarf, elf, halfling…

Get it on Google Play Become a tester

Kinstride is in closed testing. While it lasts, Google Play only shows it to testers: see how to join below.

A journey the size of a legend

  • The Long Road is 2,865 km, from the quiet farms of Hearthvale to the Cinder Peak, and then the Road Home.
  • 657 camps along the way, each with its own illustrated story, and 10 great landmarks.
  • Beyond the next landmark, the map stays hidden in fog until you get there.

Walk as you always do

  • Your daily steps from Health Connect move you forward on their own.
  • Walks, runs and hikes from your watch (Samsung Health, Google Fit, Garmin and more) count too.
  • Or start an expedition: GPS measures your pace, stride, climbs and rests. Your exact route never leaves your phone.

Your stride reveals your lineage

  • Steady and loves the climbs? Dwarf. Quick and light? Elf. Short steps with plenty of stops? Halfling.
  • Nine lineages, read from your last 4 weeks: walk differently and your lineage changes.
  • Level up from 1 to 20, from Doorstep Wanderer to The Unwearied.

Keep going

  • A daily goal in steps or distance, with a streak.
  • A weekly summary compared with last week.
  • 22 achievements: your first campfire, a half marathon, a week on the road and more.
  • Walk as a Company with friends or family: your kilometres move you together along the same road.

Free and complete

No ads and no distance limit. Kinstride Pro (US$9.99 a year, or US$24.99 once for life) adds the three rare lineages (Gnome, Goliath and Emberkin), larger companies, special postcards and everything that comes next.

Become a tester

Before Kinstride opens to everyone, Google asks for people to test it for 14 days. You need an Android phone and the Gmail account you use on Play Store.

  1. Join the test group with that Gmail account: groups.google.com/g/lince-app-testers → Join group.
  2. On your Android phone, open the Kinstride test on Google Play → Become a tester.
  3. Install Kinstride from Google Play and keep it installed for 14 days. Walk as usual; one expedition helps a lot.
  4. Tell us what you think, or anything that fails: lince.revision@gmail.com

If step 2 says the app is not available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.
