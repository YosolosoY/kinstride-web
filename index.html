KINSTRIDE

Walk. Discover who you become.

Your real steps carry you across a hand-drawn fantasy world, and the way you walk shapes your character: lineage, class, abilities and all.

Get it on Google Play Become a tester

In closed testing: for now Google Play only shows it to testers.

How it works

  1. You walk

    Daily steps count on their own. Walks from your watch count too. Or start an expedition and GPS measures your pace, stride, climbs and rests.

  2. The world opens

    Every step moves your marker across the Farwander. The fog lifts, and every few steps there is a camp with its own illustrated story.

  3. You become someone

    After three expeditions your lineage is revealed. Your habits earn a class. Walk differently and your character changes with you.

A map, not a dashboard

Nine lineages. You don’t pick one.

Kinstride reads the last four weeks of how you walk: pace, stride for your height, climbs, rests, running. Then it tells you who you walk like. And it tells you what to change if you want to become something else.

Twelve classes come from your habits: Ranger, Paladin, Monk, Barbarian, Rogue, Druid, Bard, Fighter, Wizard, Sorcerer, Warlock and Cleric. With 108 portraits, one for each lineage and class.

A character sheet you earned

Every walker gets a real sheet: your name, level, six abilities built from your walks, and the items you carry. Share it as an image, or take it to your table: it’s compatible with fifth edition.

  • Strength climbs
  • Dexterity cadence
  • Constitution endurance
  • Intelligence camps found
  • Wisdom consistency
  • Charisma walks with others

Become a tester

Before Kinstride opens to everyone, Google asks for people to test it for 14 days. You need an Android phone and the Gmail account you use on Play Store.

  1. Join the test group with that Gmail account: groups.google.com/g/lince-app-testers → Join group.
  2. On your Android phone, open the Kinstride test on Google Play → Become a tester.
  3. Install Kinstride from Google Play and keep it installed for 14 days. Walk as usual; one expedition helps a lot.
  4. Tell us what you think, or anything that fails: lince.revision@gmail.com

If step 2 says the app is not available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.
