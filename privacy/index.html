KINSTRIDE

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Versión en español

In short: Kinstride turns your walks into a journey. Your exact route never leaves your phone. Your steps stay on your phone unless you join a Company, and then only your kilometres per day are shared with its members. No ads, no selling data, no tracking.

Who is responsible

Kinstride is developed in Chile by an independent developer. For any privacy question, write to lince.revision@gmail.com.

Your height

When you start, Kinstride asks your height to understand whether your stride is short or long for you. It is stored only on your phone.

Location (expeditions)

Kinstride uses your location only while an expedition is running, which you start and end yourself. While it runs, a notification stays visible ("Expedition under way") so it can keep measuring with the screen off. Kinstride does not use your location in the background at any other time.

From the GPS points it calculates distance, time, pace, pauses and climb. The points themselves are kept only in memory during the expedition and are discarded at the end: your route is never saved and never sent anywhere. Only the summary (kilometres, minutes, steps, metres climbed) is stored on your phone.

Steps and distance (Health Connect)

If you allow it, Kinstride reads from Health Connect your steps and distance per day, starting on the day you began your journey. It does not read any other health data and never writes to Health Connect.

  • Use: to move your marker along the map, reach camps, level up and estimate the lineage your walking resembles.
  • Storage: daily totals on your phone. If you join a Company, your kilometres per day (not your steps) are sent to it.
  • Kinstride does not use this data for advertising, does not sell it and does not share it with anyone else.
  • You can remove the permission at any time in Health Connect (Kinstride › Settings › Open Health Connect).

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Companies

A Company lets up to 9 friends move one marker together. To use it, Kinstride creates an anonymous account on this phone: no email, no password, no name from your Google account. The Company stores: the name you choose to show, your lineage, your level and your kilometres per day since you joined. Only members of the same Company can see them. It is stored with Supabase, our provider, over an encrypted connection.

Purchases

Kinstride Pro is purchased through Google Play; we never see your card. To know what you bought we use RevenueCat, which receives the purchase information (product, date and status) linked to an identifier of this app.

Children

Kinstride is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Everything on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app. To delete what is stored for Companies, see Delete your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
