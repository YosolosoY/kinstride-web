Delete your data

Versión en español

Kinstride keeps almost everything on your phone. Here is how to delete each part:

What is on your phone

Your height, your journey, your expeditions and your daily steps are only on your phone. Uninstall Kinstride and they are deleted.

What is in the cloud (Companies)

If you joined a Company, the cloud holds the name you chose to show, your lineage, your level and your kilometres per day. To delete it:

  1. Open Kinstride.
  2. Go to Character and tap the gear icon (Settings).
  3. Tap Delete my cloud data and confirm.

You leave your Company and all your data there is deleted at once. A Company left with no members is deleted too.

If you no longer have the app

Write to lince.revision@gmail.com with the name you used in your Company and the Company name. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Purchases are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for payment records.
