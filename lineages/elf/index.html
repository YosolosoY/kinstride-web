Walk Like an Elf: Quick, Light and Even

Quick and light, a pace that never wavers.

Elves seem to cover ground without trying. Their pace is quick, it hardly changes from one stretch to the next, and they come back to the road almost every day.

Speed comes first. Without a quick walking pace there is no Elf. An even rhythm and steady days then say how Elf you really are.

One of the six lineages open to everyone.

What the app measures

These are the marks that give full score, taken straight from the rules of the app. You get partial score on the way there.

MeasureFull score at
Walking speed (the key)5.9 km/h or more (3.7 mph or more)
Pace changes between stretches±3 % or less
Active days90 % or more

Without the key measure there is no Elf, whatever the rest says.

Variants

With Pro, where and how you walk can make you a variant:

  • Wood Elf. You head out at dawn or through parks and nature.
  • High Elf. Your pace is nearly perfect: it changes less than 2% between stretches.
  • Nightfall Elf. You walk at night.

Not sure which one is yours? Take the walking test.
