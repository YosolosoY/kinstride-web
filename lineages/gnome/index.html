Walk Like a Gnome: Tiny, Rapid Steps

Tiny, rapid steps and a curious path.

Gnomes take more steps than anyone else on the road. Their stride is very short for their height and their cadence is very high, as if their feet had something urgent to tell the ground.

Kinstride reads only two things here: how short your stride is and how many steps you take per minute.

A rare lineage. Kinstride calculates it for everyone; Pro reveals it.

What the app measures

These are the marks that give full score, taken straight from the rules of the app. You get partial score on the way there.

MeasureFull score at
Stride length (of your height)37 % or less
Steps per minute132 or more

Variants

With Pro, where and how you walk can make you a variant:

  • Forest Gnome. Your little steps go through parks and nature.
  • Rock Gnome. Your little steps roam the city.

Not sure which one is yours? Take the walking test.
