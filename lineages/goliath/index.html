Walk Like a Goliath: Giant Strides

Giant strides for your height.

Goliaths cover in one step what others need two for. Height is not the point: Kinstride compares your stride with your own height, so anyone with a long reach can be one.

Only one measure counts here, and it is the length of your stride for your height.

A rare lineage. Kinstride calculates it for everyone; Pro reveals it.

What the app measures

These are the marks that give full score, taken straight from the rules of the app. You get partial score on the way there.

MeasureFull score at
Stride length (of your height)55 % or more

Variants

With Pro, where and how you walk can make you a variant:

  • Stone Goliath. Your strides climb hills.
  • Frost Goliath. You walk in the cold (below 8 °C / 46 °F).
  • Fire Goliath. You walk in the heat (above 26 °C / 79 °F).
  • Storm Goliath. You walk in the rain.

Not sure which one is yours? Take the walking test.
