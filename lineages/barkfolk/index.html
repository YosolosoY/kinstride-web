Walk Like Barkfolk: Slow, for Hours

Slow to start, never stops, for hours.

Barkfolk are slow to start and slower to stop. Their walking pace is unhurried, their outings last for hours, and they rarely pause, because nothing about the road makes them tired.

An unhurried pace comes first. Without it there are no Barkfolk. Long outings with very few stops then deepen the roots.

One of the six lineages open to everyone.

What the app measures

These are the marks that give full score, taken straight from the rules of the app. You get partial score on the way there.

Walking speed (the key)3.9 km/h or less (2.4 mph or less)
