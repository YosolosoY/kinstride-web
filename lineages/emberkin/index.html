Move Like Emberkin: Bursts of Fire

Fiery bursts: run, walk, run again.

Emberkin burn in bursts. They run, walk, run again, and no two stretches look the same. Calm is simply the moment before the next spark.

Kinstride looks for a share of running in the middle of your walks and a pace that keeps changing.

A rare lineage. Kinstride calculates it for everyone; Pro reveals it.
