Walk Like a Dwarf: Steady Up Every Climb

Steady as stone, loves the climbs, goes the distance.

Dwarves do not mind the hill. They prefer it. Their walks climb, their pace barely changes from the bottom to the top, and they stay out long after others have turned back.

Climbing comes first. Without real ascent there is no Dwarf. An even pace and long outings then add to it.

One of the six lineages open to everyone.

What the app measures

These are the marks that give full score, taken straight from the rules of the app. You get partial score on the way there.

MeasureFull score at
Climb (the key)24 m / km or more (127 ft / mi or more)
Pace changes between stretches±4 % or less
Length of each outing120 min or more

Without the key measure there is no Dwarf, whatever the rest says.
