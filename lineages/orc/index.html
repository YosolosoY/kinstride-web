Move Like an Orc: All In, Running

All in, hard from start to finish.

Orcs do not stroll. They run most of the way, and they run hard from the first minute to the last.

Running comes first: without it there is no Orc. Overall speed and a sustained effort then show how strong that run really is.

One of the six lineages open to everyone.

What the app measures

These are the marks that give full score, taken straight from the rules of the app. You get partial score on the way there.

MeasureFull score at
Time jogging (the key)75 % or more
Speed on your outings10 km/h or more (6.2 mph or more)
Pace changes between stretches±5 % or less

Without the key measure there is no Orc, whatever the rest says.
