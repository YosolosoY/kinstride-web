Nine lineages, nine ways of walking

You don’t pick your lineage in Kinstride. The app reads four weeks of your walks and tells you who you walk like. When your walking changes, your lineage changes too.

Halfling

Short, lively steps, unhurried walks with plenty of stops.

Dwarf

Steady as stone, loves the climbs, goes the distance.

Human

Does it all: climbs, speed, distance and running. The hardest lineage to earn.

Elf

Quick and light, a pace that never wavers.

Orc

All in, hard from start to finish.

Barkfolk

Slow to start, never stops, for hours.

Gnome

Tiny, rapid steps and a curious path.

Goliath

Giant strides for your height.

Emberkin

Fiery bursts: run, walk, run again.

Not sure which one is yours? Take the walking test.

Kinstride is in closed testing on Android. Test it for 14 days and see what your real walks say.
