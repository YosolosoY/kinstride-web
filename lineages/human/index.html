Walk Like a Human: The Hardest Lineage to Earn

Does it all: climbs, speed, distance and running. The hardest lineage to earn.

Humans refuse to be just one thing. A hard climb one day, a fast walk the next, a long outing on Saturday, a run when nobody expected it.

This is the hardest lineage in Kinstride. It asks for four different feats within four weeks, plenty of outings and steady habits. Nobody starts as a Human: you have to walk your way there.

One of the six lineages open to everyone.

What the app measures

These are the marks that give full score, taken straight from the rules of the app. You get partial score on the way there.

MeasureFull score at
Feats: a hard climb (20 m per km), walking at 6 km/h, 75 minutes and running (the key)4 of 4
Outings in 4 weeks10 or more
Active days50 % or more

Without the key measure there is no Human, whatever the rest says.
