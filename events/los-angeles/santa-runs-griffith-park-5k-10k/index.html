Santa Runs Griffith Park (5K/10K) 2026

A fifth-annual 5K and 10K run where participants wear Santa suits alongside hundreds of other Santas on a flat and fast course. It also has a Kids Dash, and finishers receive a custom medal, with chip timing, music, and on-site sports massage.
