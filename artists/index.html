Versión en español

Kinstride is an Android walking game. Every image in it (creatures, scenes, travellers, portraits, objects) can be drawn by a human illustrator, with the artist’s name on every piece. If you are an illustrator and want real work in a published app, this page explains how.

Why we are doing this

Kinstride is an independent project. Today its images were generated with AI, and we want to replace them with human work. You get real, published work with a brief, a style guide and review. Later, users will be able to choose which artist’s drawings they see in the app, or see a mix of all of them.

What you get

  • Credit on every piece: your name and Instagram handle on each of your images and on the postcards users share.
  • An “Artists” page in the app and on this site, with your link.
  • A signed letter confirming the collaboration: what you did, how many pieces, the date and the Google Play link.

Your work counts as “published” when a version that includes it reaches production on Google Play. We will tell you the date.

Style: Kinstride identity, your touch

  • Fixed: field-notebook look, ink or marker sketch; black and greys only (the app inks it in its own colour, day and night); plain white background; no text or signature inside the image; a recognisable subject with personality.
  • Yours: your line, hatching, proportions, stylisation, humour, pose and details.
  • Delivery: PNG, 3:4 (min. 1800×2400 px) for creatures, travellers and portraits; 4:5 (1800×2250) for scenes; 1:1 (1536×1536) for objects.
  • Not allowed: characters or creatures from other franchises, logos, brands, and generative AI.

Terms in short

  • You own your work and may show it in your portfolio, social media and CV.
  • Kinstride may use it in the app, the store, this site and its promotion, always with your credit. The licence is non-exclusive.
  • Only technical changes are made (ink colour, crop, size).
  • Your art is never used to train AI.

This is a summary; the collaboration is confirmed in a short signed licence.

How to join

  1. Press the button below. It opens a Google Drive folder with the style guide (PDF), the list of pieces and reference images.
  2. The folder is private. Sign in with your Google account and press Request access; add your name and Instagram handle in the message.
  3. When access is granted you will see the guide and the references.
